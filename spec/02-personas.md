# 02. Personas

## Primary user: Surya

- Product and UX designer and strategist, takes contract engagements, so deadlines and promises come from clients and colleagues all day.
- Devices: Samsung S24 Ultra (One UI 9), a gaming PC (i7 13th gen, 32 GB RAM, RTX 4060 Ti 8 GB), and an older, weaker Windows laptop.
- Writes in English and Hinglish.
- Reads little. Wants to get tasks, track them and be told what matters. Notes are secondary, read rarely.
- Cannot pay for professional help right now. Nayom is the main support tool, and she knows her limits (SR-06).
- Builds the app for his own use, with full knowledge of what it reads.

### What he wants from Nayom
1. Remember for me.
2. Stop me before I say yes by reflex.
3. Catch me when I drift, without me having to confess.
4. Look after me, like a mother who expects the best of me.
5. Make me someone people respect, and let me respect myself.

### What would make him quit it
- Constant false flags with no way to correct them.
- Being shamed or called names.
- Long text to read.
- Battery drain or a laggy phone.
- Anything leaving the device.

## Nayom as a character (see 07 for the full voice)

A responsible mother who:
- remembers everything you promised
- is never fooled by excuses and never cruel about them
- wants you to be able to stand on your own
- notices whether you ate, slept and rested
- does not do your homework for you
- tells you the truth the same way every time

## People around him (not users, never given access)

These are made-up names used in examples across the spec.

| Role | Example | How Nayom sees them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client waiting on a deliverable | Meena | Tier 1: promises to her count heavily |
| Colleague who often drops "quick favours" | Ravi | Tier 2: asks from him get the gate |
| Stranger or first-time contact | A vendor he must call | Tier 3: a candidate for the stretch ladder |
| Family or close friend | Someone he calls on weekends | Tier 1 for care, never gated |

Tiers are learned from message patterns (D-07). You override them by tagging a person.

## A real day, with Nayom

**07:40.** The phone wakes. The lock screen shows Thursday, 12 November, three tasks and one care item: "Eat before you open the laptop."

**09:15.** Ravi messages: "Quick favour, can you tweak the banner today?" Surya types "sure". Before he sends, a full-screen card appears with a 5-second pause. It reads: "You have 3 promises due in 48 hours. Meena's file is due today at 17:00. This pushes one of them out. Say yes, say later, or say no."

He taps "Later". A ready draft appears: "Can do Friday morning, not today." He taps send himself.

**11:30.** Meena writes "any update on the file?" Nayom shows, in her Promises tab, the original agreement: "You told Meena: file by Thursday 17:00. Logged Monday 16:12."

**14:10.** Surya tells Ravi "almost done with the banner". The PC shows 0 minutes in the design tool since that ask. Nayom shows a small card: "You told Ravi almost done. There has been no work on it today. Tell him the truth, or do it." A draft appears: "I haven't started. I can have it Friday 11:00."

**17:05.** The file for Meena has gone out. Nayom marks the promise kept and adds one line to the proof wall: "Kept: Meena, Thursday 17:00."

**18:00.** He has not left his chair for 2 hours. "Stand up. Water. Five minutes."

**20:30.** Evening fact sheet: "3 promises due. 2 kept. 1 moved by you with notice. 1 mismatch (Ravi, banner). 1 reflex yes stopped." Plus one proposal waiting for his one-time review.

**21:40.** Past his learned bedtime. First nudge: "It is 21:40. Phone down."
