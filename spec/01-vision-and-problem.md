# 01. Vision and problem

## Vision

Nayom is the second mother you wish you had on hand every hour. She remembers what you agreed to. She notices when you drift. She tells you the plain truth, calmly, and then asks what you will do about it. She never pities you and never rescues you. Over time she steps back, because the aim is that you no longer need her.

## The problem, in plain words

These come from your own notes. They are grouped by what actually goes wrong.

### A. Memory and agreements
- You forget what was discussed soon after the talk ends, sometimes within minutes.
- You forget what you agreed with someone earlier.
- You do not hold a clear picture of today: the date, the day, what is due.

### B. Priority and people-pleasing
- New asks get the same priority as things already agreed, or higher.
- Certain people's tasks jump the queue so that you can avoid an awkward moment.
- You do work that is not your job.
- You say yes first and think afterwards.

### C. Honesty and thinking
- You say things that are not true yet, then work hard to make them true.
- You feel unable to think clearly and straight. That pushes you toward cheaper shortcuts that waste time later.
- You sometimes feel more capable than the evidence shows.

### D. Anxiety and avoidance
- You fear letting someone down once a thing is entrusted to you.
- Talking to people feels heavy, strangers most of all.
- Even a small change in comfort zone brings strong anxiety.
- You avoid situations, needed or not, and the people and tasks connected to them.

### E. Focus and interest
- Too many distractions, strong impulses and too many iterations.
- Work loses your interest when it is long, complex, rushed, unrewarded, unrecognised, stuck in approvals, or involves an abrasive person.

### F. Self-care
- Health and self-care get dropped. Work is put before everything else.

## What Nayom does about each

| Problem area | Nayom's answer | Features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Captures every agreement the moment it is made, recalls it on demand | FR-01 to FR-05 |
| B | Stops you before a reflex yes, shows what it pushes out, offers a firm no | FR-10 to FR-13 |
| C | Compares what you say with what you said before and what you did | FR-30 to FR-32, FR-52 |
| D | Spots avoidance, builds a daily one-step ladder, shows proof of progress | FR-50, FR-51, FR-60 |
| E | Watches distraction, cuts the day to three tasks | FR-40, FR-41 |
| F | One care item every day, break and sleep nudges | FR-42, FR-44 |

## Goals

1. Every agreement you make is written down within one minute, with no effort from you.
2. You can see in one glance what is due today and this week.
3. Fewer reflex yeses. Target, tracked weekly: the share of new asks you accept inside 60 seconds drops each month.
4. Promises kept on time rise week over week, and Nayom's reminders per promise fall (D-20).
5. You do one small uncomfortable action a day.
6. Nothing leaves your own devices.

## Non-goals

- Not a therapist and not medical care. She says so and points you to people (SR-06).
- Not a surveillance tool for anyone else. One owner, own devices.
- Not a to-do app for other people's goals. She holds you to your promises and your own rules (D-19).
- No calls or in-person talk recorded. The only audio is your own voice note when you tap debrief (FR-03).
- No auto-sending messages (D-11).
- No reading of banking, finance, OTP, authenticator or password apps (D-12).

## How we will know it works

Weekly, measured by Nayom from the vault with no self-report:

| Measure | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Promises kept on time ÷ promises due | up |
| New asks accepted inside 60 s | down |
| Mismatches flagged per week | down |
| Avoided messages older than their limit | down |
| Reminders needed per kept promise | down |
| Days with a stretch completed | up |
| Days with the care item done | up |
