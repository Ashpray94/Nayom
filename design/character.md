# Nayom: character sheet (D-32)

Rule: **head only, or full body. Never a floating head with a half body.**
- Head only: portraits and the app icon (stern, worried, wry, firm, hello, icon).
- Full body: every scene (empty states). She is always standing or sitting on a surface, never floating.

## Fixed features (never vary)
- Head: wide oval, beige skin, soft matte vinyl, no nose, no ears except the side ear lights.
- Hair: one thick solid black hair cap with a single round bun on top. No strands, no fringe, no green.
- Ear lights: one mint-green glowing capsule on each side of the head.
- Eyes: solid black squarish-rounded, one small white highlight each. Long upper lash lines act as the brows. No separate eyebrows, no lower lid, no dots inside.
- Mouth: thin dark-green line, small. Changes per mood only.
- Blush: none. Glasses: none. Jewellery: none.

## Body (full-body scenes)
- Proportion: adult woman, calm and capable. Head about 1/4 of total height, slim shoulders, narrow waist, no exaggerated chibi.
- Outfit: one soft mint-green tunic with a long straight hem to the shin, three-quarter sleeves, a simple round neckline. Same fabric as the matte vinyl, one colour only, no patterns.
- Hands: simple rounded mitten hands, four fingers implied, same beige as the face.
- Legs and feet: small rounded beige shoes, hidden when seated.
- Posture: upright, relaxed, slightly leaning toward what she attends to. Never sprawled.

## Rendering
Soft 3D matte vinyl, soft studio light, mint-white background fading to transparent. Props are simple and few, one concept colour only. No text. Square canvas, subject centred with room to crop.
